Classify the function

Decide whether each function is convex, concave, both (that is, affine) or neither on the stated domain. After each answer you get the one-line argument: a second-derivative check or a rule from slide 7. Most of these are new; they are not the ones from the slides. Slides 6–21

Name the problem class

For each problem, choose the smallest class from the lecture that it belongs to, possibly after a standard reformulation such as the epigraph trick. Choose “Not convex” when no convex formulation exists. Recall the hierarchy LP ⊂ QP ⊂ QCQP ⊂ SOCP ⊂ SDP. Slides 29–38

Proof practice

Each statement comes from the lecture; several are left as “Why?” or “proof?” on the slides. Try the proof on paper first. Then reveal a hint, or reveal the proof one step at a time and compare.
